Danily Trader

A private trading assistant, built and operated by Danial Darabi for his own trading accounts.

Danily Trader is a personal software tool. It helps me follow gold (XAUUSD) trade ideas from signal channels I subscribe to, and trades them on my own broker accounts with strict, fixed risk rules. It is not a public service and it is not offered to other people. It does not manage anyone else's money.

What it does

  1. Reads new trade ideas for gold (XAUUSD), and their later updates, from signal channels I follow.
  2. Turns each message into a structured order proposal: direction, entry zone, stop loss and take profits.
  3. Checks every proposal against fixed rules before anything is sent: a stop loss is required, the entry must be close to the current price, and the position size comes from a fixed risk percentage.
  4. Places, modifies and closes orders on my own demo and live accounts through the cTrader Open API.
  5. Sends me a short report of every opened and closed trade, and a daily summary of results.

Safety rules

  • Every new strategy runs on a demo account first; live trading needs a separate, explicit switch.
  • No order is ever placed without a stop loss.
  • Hard limits on position size, number of open trades and daily loss.
  • Only my own accounts are used. Access can be revoked at any time.
